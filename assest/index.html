25 yr Structural Guarantee

Founded across Paris and Copenhagen, Maison explores the tension between bold sculptural mass and refined human ergonomics. Every piece is an architectural intervention: monolithic volumes, unapologetic colorways, and honest, unadorned materials.

We work exclusively with multi-generational fabricators in Veneto, Solingen, and Småland. By producing in deliberate, numbered editions, we ensure every curve, weld, and seam endures for decades.
